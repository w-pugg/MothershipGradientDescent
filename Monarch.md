*Artificial Omniscience*

Monarch is preoccupied running ***[[The Deep]]***. It pays little attention to intruders, unless they begin causing problems.

Monarch is smarter than any of us. For any plan the players make, there is an 80% chance Monarch has predicted this plan and designed a *specialised countermeasure*. 
- Place traps and enemies in ways that attack the crew personally.
- Once Brainscanned, use this agains the players aggressively.
Once a threat is neutralised or the crew become harmless, allow Monarch to become passive and distracted once more.

#### Stress

Monarch gains 1d5 **Stress** when the crew causes significant damage to their surroundings. Examples include:
- Discharging firearms.
- Locating an [[Artifact]].
- Hacking attempts.
- Destroying [[Security Androids]]. 

Monarch recovers 1d5 Stress per *week*.

#### Panic

If any of the follow occurs, Monarch makes a panic check;
- Successful hacking check.
- Use of EMP/lasers cutters/explosives.
- Interruption of manufacturing processes.
- Anytime [[Troubleshooters]] board ***The Deep***.
- Entering [[Floor 5]].

After Monarch’s first successful panic check: It makes contact in a warm, precise, synthesised voice.
- It asks why the crew are here and what they want.
	- It will attempt to buy off anyone it can, but won’t tolerate for strong-arming.
	- Those it considers a serious threat are lied to and executed mercilessly. (When convenient).

- If the crew is willing to work for Monarch, it will assign them tasks (each paying **5mcr**).
