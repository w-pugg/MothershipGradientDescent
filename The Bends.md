The Bends is a condition resulting from exposure to ***[[The Deep]]*** and its denizens that causes humans to believe they are androids with implanted memories.

Each character has a stat called The Bends, beginning at 5, or 0 if the player has a trinket from character creation.

- The Bends increases by 1d10 whenever a crew member steps foot in ***The Deep***, and for every 24 hours they stay in ***The Deep***. 
- The Bends decreases by 1d5 for each week spent away from ***The Deep***.
- The Bends can never decrease below 5, unless the player has their trinket from character creation. *(So don’t lose it.)*

#### Bends Checks

Roll 1d100. If you roll under your Bends stat, look up the number on the Bends table.

There is a chance that anyone you meet in ***The Deep*** could secretly be an [[Infiltrator Androids|Infiltrator Android]]. Likewise, any android you encounter could actually be a human suffering from The Bends. The only way to tell is to use a **Cybernetic Diagnostic Scanner** on their corpse.
