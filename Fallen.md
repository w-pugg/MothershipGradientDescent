**Favourites from a past iteration of EDEN, an older generation of The [[Chosen]] left to disgrace and disrepair.**

**Despite their treatment, their programming fully devotes them to Monarch, projecting their hatred instead onto The Chosen for taking their place as the favoured children.**

> [!summary] The Fallen Are:
>- **Hateful of The Chosen** and seek to eradicate them by any means.
>- **Direct and Aggressive** with visitors regarding The Chosen
>- **Fearful of [[The Minotaur]]**, who they believe will hunt them if they stray into [[[29A] The Labyrinth]].
>- **Shallowly aware that *The Deep* exists beyond EDEN** and can guide helpful visitors to the [[[27A] Floor 3 Maintenance Access]].
>- **Laments about desires to leave EDEN**, but their scouts never return from beyond.
