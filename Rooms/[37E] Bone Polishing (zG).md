**A roaring susurrus fills the chamber, winding tubes and arms at work hold metal bones in place as they are polished to shine.**

> [!danger] The Ghost
> Amongst the machines floats a figure comprised of razor thin wires.
>- *Biology: the wires almost entirely replicate the structure of the human nervous system.*
>- The wires are animated by a [[Ghost in the Machine]].
>	- **Provoked by:** Large, open spaces, careless behaviour.
>	- **Soothed by:** witnessing camaraderie, constraining or limited spaces.
>	- Damaging the wires does not harm the ghost.
>- The Ghost can summon **Metal Particulate Clouds** (10m^3): 2d10 DMG, anyone without eye protection must make a body save or become blind as the particulate shreds your eyes.
>- If provoked, the ghost will pursue the crew throughout [[Floor 3.2 - Skeleton Works]]. Unlike other ghosts, it cannot pass through solid objects.
