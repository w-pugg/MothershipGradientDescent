**EDEN is a prolonged experiment run by [[Monarch]]. **

**Confined from the rest of *[[The Deep]]*, the two groups of androids that live here, The [[Chosen]] and The [[Fallen]] are programmed to think EDEN is the entire universe and Monarch their god. Once the experiment has run its course, Monarch will wipe all the androids’ brains and begin again.**
