Troubleshooters are the marines to the Navy of the Blockade. A private army tasked with boarding ***[[The Deep]]*** and ensuring no [[Infiltrator Androids]] escape with little concern for collateral damage.

The Naval Blockade surrounding ***The Deep*** contracts all sorts of vessels equipped for interdiction, however, due to the detail being a shit one, this results in an ineffective cordon riddled with loopholes and corruption.
