Found within [[[46C] Cleanroom (zG)]], Containment Suits are full-body wear designed to prevent matter passing between from the wearer to their environment, and vice versa. It is made of a specialised nylon blend fabric resistant to the decontamination chemicals utilised by the [[[33A] Decontamination (zG)]] room that would otherwise corrode or deteriorate vaccsuits and similar equipment.
