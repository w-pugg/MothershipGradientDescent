# Basic Procedures 
Surprise yourself.

*Random Encounters* provide context to the spaces, *Reaction Rolls* give meaning to the interactions.
Resist the temptation to turn these encounters into fights. Everything in ***[[The Deep]]*** wants to talk to someone.

#### Random Encounters

Roll 1d100 once in each :LiSquareDashed:Human Scale room / thrice in each :LiSquare:Industrial Scale room, as well as after loud noise.

On *Doubles*, an encounter occurs. 
- Low numbers mean the encounter is close by.
- High numbers mean the encounter is far away.

#### Reaction Rolls

Roll 1d100 to determine the encounter’s disposition toward the crew.
- Evens means a positive reaction, odd means negative.
- Rolling Doubles amplifies the strength of the reaction (positive :LiArrowBigRight: friendly, negative :LiArrowBigRight: hostile.) 
- High rolls indicate the encounter is advantaged in some way. Low rolls mean the contrary.

#### Zero-G

Don’t sweat it unless the stakes are high, or the players find it interesting.
- Anyone with Zero-G are fine, any without suffer [-]
- Momentum doesn’t go anywhere, it must be cancelled.
- Due to the size of ***The Deep***, players may lose any points of reference. This should cause Stress and be horrible.
- Pressurised gas can be used as a propellant.

#### Brainscans

**[[Monarch]]** hoards comprehensive backups of human brains, called [[Brainscans]], which it uses to create human seeming [[Infiltrator Androids]]. 

The Deep is full of places the crew can be brainscanned.

Brainscans are stored at the [[[47H] Brainscan Databank]].
Monarch will create Infiltrator Androids of the crew using their Brainscans, if it feels it is necessary.
Monarch knows all of the most intimate memories of Brainscan victims and will use them relentlessly.
Getting Brainscanned increases your **[[The Bends|Bends]]** by 1d10.
