
The [[Brainscans]] [[Monarch]] harvests are endlessly forked, merged, and trained. Occasionally, half-deleted Brainscans can “break off” and fuse with holographic light-phenomena creating a Ghost in the Machine: a self-perpetuating construct present on the visible spectrum without mass, doomed to wander ***[[The Deep]]*** forever.

- Appears like a looped animation when encountered, fixed in an endless spasm of discomfort.
- When encountered, will wail and moan about their trauma.
- If provoked, the ghost will attack
	- They only stop attacking if soothed, *will pursue for 1d10 rooms*.
	- They pass through all physical barriers but are impeded by most radiation and radio waves.
