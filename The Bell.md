The Bell is an old retrofitted thruster module discarded by ***[[The Deep]]*** a long time ago. It is inhabited by [[Arkady]], [[Noriko]], and rarely [[Ghost Eater]]. From time to time, divers in need of rest can be found here as well, though most are never seen again.
